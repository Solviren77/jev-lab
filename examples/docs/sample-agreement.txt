SERVICES AGREEMENT between Acme Corp ("Customer") and Northwind LLC ("Provider").
1. Term. The initial term is thirty-six (36) months. The agreement renews automatically for successive
twelve-month terms unless either party gives ninety (90) days' written notice before the end of the term.
2. Termination. Customer may terminate early only on payment of the fees remaining for the current term.
3. Indemnity. Customer shall indemnify and hold Provider harmless against third-party claims arising
from Customer's data.
4. Governing law. This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.
